# Story — pharmacy record fields

**As** the pharmacy desk, **I want** a medicine to carry its HSN code, its pack label and how many loose units a pack
holds, each batch to carry the MRP printed on it, a supplier to carry its email and address, and a purchase order to
carry a note, **so that** bills print what the GST return needs, orders and statements reach the supplier, and payment
terms are not lost.
